# Definition
_Deus ex machina_ means God from the machine. It is a tongue-in-cheek theater trope in which the conflict is resolved by an act of (the) god(s). 
> 
> The mechanics: in ancient theater, the machine was an actor attached to a rig to portray them as a god figure, and that god figure comes out of (presumably) stage left to fix all the problems.

This literary device is still used today as a crutch—to resolve conflict in an unexpected and unsatisfying way.
